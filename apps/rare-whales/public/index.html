WHAT COULD GO WRONG?

Newly minted rewards are not revenue. Unlimited emissions can overwhelm demand; claim fees can make small rewards uneconomic. Perks should add convenience and personality, without multiplying rewards or changing competitive trading rules.

If staking is added later, it moves an NFT into a contract. Depositors must keep their access, get a safe withdrawal path, and avoid double use of the same whale. Rewards should accrue only while staked; the exit flow should settle already-earned rewards before withdrawal.

A profitable historical replay does not qualify a pool to manage money. Real pooled trading introduces losses, custody, withdrawals, share pricing, conflicts and legal obligations. That is a separate future product.
