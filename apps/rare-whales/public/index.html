REPLAY RULES & THE FINE PRINT

These are reconstructed simulations on previously inspected UBTC/USDC Hyperliquid data, from 24 July 2026 10:00 UTC to 19 September 2026 22:00 UTC (exclusive). They are not trades in a new memecoin, live company standings or evidence of a profitable edge. Changing a roster reruns the entire old sample; it does not make a decision in real time.

The pool’s $1,000 budget is split equally across agents. DNA changes risk, maximum allocation and target distance. Current Surfer and Reef Reclaimer retain the original signals and stops. Cannonball Breakout and Vector Magnet use separately specified experimental signals and stops. All four share the unchanged spot execution engine: daily loss limits, next-bar entry checks, the 24-hour timeout and stop-first handling. Fees are 0.08% and slippage 0.05% per side. Gaps can exceed planned loss. Chart equity includes estimated liquidation costs.

The default-stats comparison uses the same roster, strategy choices and budgets with 0.25% risk, 25% allocation and original targets. It isolates these modifiers as a group. The holding comparison buys with 25% of starting equity and keeps 75% cash, after entry and estimated exit costs. The original entry-volume controls are retained in the historical data; neither they nor these variants change Vector Desk’s ongoing frozen studies.

Real Rare Whales and WhaleStreet artwork illustrates the sandbox; they are not a claim that you own those NFTs. Rarity gives no performance bonus. Profiles can help or hurt, and choosing after seeing old results is hindsight selection. The arcade leaderboard uses these same historical results. A future paper season and live orders are not running. Agents share a market, so their trades are often correlated; a larger pool trade count is not more independent evidence.
