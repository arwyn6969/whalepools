## Observed session

1. Start an assigned local report before creating the first live company. Use one browser/wallet. An existing-watch session cannot establish first activation.
2. Sign in and wait for complete verified inventory. Start the stopwatch when inventory is ready; mark help honestly.
3. Choose owned whales and a preset, name the company, read public consent and confirm Start. Observe whether the holder can explain the queued action or wait, simulated capital and costs.
4. Open another watch and a saved UTC day; return to the private setup. Test Share this day, Copy day link and PNG/SVG. Inspect the chosen destination manually. A share sheet or output alone does not prove a post was delivered or opened.
5. On a later UTC day within seven days of first Start, deliberately update/review newer observations on the own watch or confirm a follow-up watch. Automatic shared-day landing, page views, outputs and Tide picks alone do not count. The participant panel explains their local progress and window deadline.

Save only anonymised session code, device/browser, UTC time, unaided/helped, creation duration and failure theme in a private session sheet. Record real checks separately from synthetic fixture evidence. Collect five interviews: what makes the company yours; what the last wait/decision meant; what would bring you back; whether shared-day competition helps; and what required help. Compare existing Daily Tide with the proposed Safe Harbour brief without implying the latter is available.
