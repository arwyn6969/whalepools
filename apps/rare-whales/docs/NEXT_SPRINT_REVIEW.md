# Sharing, Tidal Trio and holder pilot candidate

Prepared 6 October 2026 on `codex/share-challenge-pilot`, stacked on the open holder journey draft PR #2 (`ec7fa3d`). Maintenance draft PR #1 remains separate. This is a review candidate, not a production or remote staging release.

Public companies have stable `#company/<id>` destinations. The server looks up any published company, including outside the top 100, and returns public fields with the historical dates, rank and rules hash. An edit retains the ID; withdrawal returns 404. Republishing after withdrawal creates a fresh destination. Open the company from the board or My Company, copy its URL, download its dated SVG card, compare with the local private crew, or inspect its replay. Comparison renders locally; exports contain only the fetched public company. The card deliberately uses text identities without substituted NFT portraits. This is an exportable SVG, not an automatic social preview or posting integration.

The separately versioned `tidal-trio-v1` challenge drafts exactly three of six loaners and assigns any of the four existing tactics. Each episode resets to $1,000 split equally. Causal signals retain the original historical warmup; only signals inside each window enter, and the frozen engine closes positions at the window end. Episodes are 24 July 10:00–22 August 00:00 UTC and 22 August 00:00–19 September 22:00 UTC, with end-exclusive boundaries. Market price changes are +20.37% and +3.51%. A crew must complete a trade in each episode and keep both drawdowns at or below 0.25%; its cleared score is its lower episode return. Current choices and the best cleared crew are saved under the exact challenge hash, separately from sandbox/holder drafts.

All 1,280 legal choices are enumerated. 537 clear the declared brief; six tie for the best lower return of approximately +0.168147%. No choice dominates every other choice on return and drawdown in both episodes. The top 50 deterministic choices contain 50 Current Surfer, 49 Reef Reclaimer, 51 Cannonball Breakout and zero Vector Magnet assignments. Ties use enumeration order for this diagnostic count; this is not a public ranking tie-break. This does not prove game balance. Vector Magnet's usefulness and repeated play remain questions for the pilot. The initial 1% drawdown budget did not constrain choices, so 0.25% was selected after enumeration. All of this is inspected-history game design, not a new independent strategy study.

Pilot recording is opt-in and local, off by default. Reports whitelist an assigned P01–P10 code, event names/times and assistance flag; no wallet, roster, company name, signature or session data. No endpoint collects events. Browser records expire in 30 days and cap at 500 events. The operator CLI aggregates voluntarily exported reports and refuses duplicate codes. [HOLDER_PILOT.md](HOLDER_PILOT.md) defines recruitment, tasks, retention, ten-holder gates, five interviews and a seven-day return readout. No real pilot outcomes are claimed.
