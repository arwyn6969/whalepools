# Live holder pilot v1

Prepared 7 October 2026. Zero observed participants. This is separate from [the historical pilot](HOLDER_PILOT.md); fixture exports verify software and must never enter real results.

Before invitations, finish real eligible-wallet desktop/mobile acceptance and the 7–14-day recorder rehearsal. The organiser recruits ten eligible first-live-company holders and privately assigns P01–P10. Keep contact/code mappings, wallets and voluntary reports outside Git/public assets. No invitation or social post is sent by this sprint.

Participant brief: open `#live-pilot`, start a local report before creating your first live company, and use one browser/wallet. Sign in, wait for verified inventory, choose a preset/default owned crew, name the company and confirm Start. Explain what the preset is waiting for, how its costs work and why paper results differ from the historical replay. Inspect another watch, return to your own, and optionally download a dated card or choose tomorrow's Daily Tide preset. Mark assistance honestly.

Return on a later UTC calendar day, within seven days of the confirmed first Start. On your own watch click REVIEW NEW OBSERVATION after a newer completed observation appears, or confirm a new follow-up watch after stopping the previous one. Page views, sharing and joining a round alone do not satisfy meaningful return. A share event means obtaining an output, not proof someone posted/opened it. All picks for a preset follow one public executor; Daily Tide does not create independent strategy trials.

Export the report voluntarily through your usual organiser contact; keep only the latest export per code. Reports are off by default, local only, expire after thirty days, and accept at most 500 events. No wallet, company name, roster, run ID, signature or session enters the export. Switching wallet stops recording. Export or erase the previous report before another participant; reports do not sync across browsers. Storage failure is visible and does not block company controls.

Aggregate voluntary files with:

```sh
node scripts/live-pilot-readout.mjs /path/to/P01.json /path/to/P02.json
```

The CLI accepts only `holder-live-pilot-v1`, whitelists fields and rejects duplicate codes/future events. Activation requires verified new-holder readiness then a confirmed first live Start. Existing watches are excluded from first activation. Proposed gates: at least eight of ten unaided starts; median unaided creation below 180 seconds after inventory is ready; five distinct meaningful later-day returns within seven days; five interviews. Missing participants remain missing; let every return window elapse. Reports are voluntary client self-reports, not verified attribution or profitability evidence. Use organiser observation to resolve repeated codes, browser resets or participant changes. Retain volunteered files outside Git; delete them thirty days after collection.

Interview five participants without coaching: “What made this company yours?”, “What did the last decision or wait mean?”, “What would bring you back?”, “Did the shared day make choosing a preset interesting?”, “What confused you or needed help?” Record anonymised themes and aggregates only. Separate enjoyment/usability, execution integrity and strategy performance. A short cohort cannot establish a profitable edge.

The follow-up sprint should use these observations to improve sharing and day-end summaries. Any change to challenge execution/objectives receives a separate version and prospective start; preserve existing Daily Tide/paper records and historical ranks.
