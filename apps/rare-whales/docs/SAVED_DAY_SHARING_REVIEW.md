# Saved-day sharing and pilot preparation · 8 October 2026

Holders can share a specific UTC day's saved decisions rather than only a changing watch balance. A canonical `watch/<uuid>?day=YYYY-MM-DD` opens that recap automatically, supplies dated server metadata and a readable no-JavaScript fallback, and preserves both private draft scopes. Invalid dates return 400; unavailable days return 404. Day previews read the public name and bounded recap instead of decoding the full watch twice.

Share this day invokes native URL/text sharing directly from the click, as required by [the Web Share API](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/Navigator/share). Unsupported/denied sharing falls back to copying or a selectable link. Cancellation never copies or records an output. Successful sheets tell the holder to confirm delivery in the chosen app; no post or recipient is inferred. PNG/SVG cards preserve the displayed day, window, balance change, fills, costs, actionable/missing coverage and full rules hash. A day still unfolding and partial/waiting data remain clear. These are paper valuations, not realized profit or evidence of strategy edge.

Late read/share replies cannot alter another wallet/route or a newer day snapshot. Automatic day landing never counts as a pilot return. Existing explicit newer own-watch review/follow-up rules and report version remain frozen. The participant panel explains first Start, assistance, meaningful later-day review and the actual seven-day deadline. Reports remain optional/local, with no new export fields or automatic transmission.
